COMMUNITY WITHDRAWAL GUIDE

From ACU
to PHP

Turn your Acurast rewards into pesos — step by step.

Received some ACU for providing compute on Acurast and want it in your GCash, Maya or bank account? This guide walks the whole route in four steps, with a screenshot for every tap.

ACU→Gate→USDT→PHP→GCash · Maya · Bank

BEFORE YOU START

What you need

Acurast Hub account with ACU

Where your rewards land. Sign in at hub.acurast.com and check your ACU balance.

A Gate account

The exchange you'll route through — based on community feedback, the easiest and cheapest route. You need it verified and ready to receive a deposit.

A PHP payment method

GCash, Maya, or a bank account — whatever you want the pesos to arrive in.

READ THIS BEFORE STEP 4

In P2P trading you hold the USDT until the buyer pays. Never confirm or release the transaction in Gate until you have opened GCash, Maya or your bank app and seen the money arrive. Once you confirm, the USDT is gone.

1

STEP ONE

Deposit ACU to Gate

First, get your ACU deposit address from Gate. You're not sending anything yet — you're just fetching the address your ACU will be sent to.

  1. Open the Gate app.
  2. Tap Assets — bottom-right of the nav bar.
  3. Then tap Deposit, first in the action row.
  4. Choose Onchain Deposit — crypto coming in from another wallet.
  5. Search for ACU and pick the result labelled Acurast.

Select the Acurast network, then copy your ACU deposit address.

NOTE
Make sure the deposit network matches the network you're sending from. Sending ACU on the wrong network can lose the funds permanently.
DON'T RETYPE IT
Always copy and paste the address, then check the first and last few characters match. One wrong character and the ACU goes somewhere nobody can recover it from.

2

STEP TWO

Transfer ACU from the Acurast Hub to Gate

Now send the ACU — from the Hub to the address you just copied.
Open the Acurast Hub — hub.acurast.com — and go to your ACU balance.

  1. Select Transfer.
  2. Paste the Gate ACU deposit address, enter the amount, and confirm.
NOTE
Don't transfer your full ACU balance — leave some behind for gas fees, which is why the screenshot sends 8 of 10.96 ACU instead of tapping MAX. Then wait for the ACU to appear in Gate.

3

STEP THREE

Trade ACU for USDT

ACU doesn't sell straight into pesos, so first swap it for USDT — the dollar stablecoin that PHP buyers actually trade against.

  1. In Gate, open Trade → Spot Trading.
  2. Search for the ACU/USDT pair.
  3. Select Sell, enter the amount of ACU you want to sell, and confirm the order.
TIP
A Limit order only fills at the price you set. If you want out immediately, switch the order type to Market and you take whatever the current price is.

4

STEP FOUR

Sell USDT for PHP

The last hop. In Gate go to P2P → Sell USDT, pick PHP, and choose your preferred payment method. You're selling to a real person, so read every step here.

  1. Navigate to the P2P section.
  2. Pick the Sell option.
  3. Choose USDT.
  4. Select PHP.
  5. Choose your preferred payment method.
  6. Select an available buyer.
  7. Enter the amount and place the order.
  8. Wait for the buyer to send the payment to your selected payment method.
  9. Check your GCash, Maya, or bank account to confirm the payment has arrived.
  10. Only after confirming the payment, complete and confirm the transaction in Gate.
NOTE
Available payment methods can vary, so choose whichever option is available and convenient for you.

Once the order is placed the buyer has a fixed window to pay — 15 minutes in the screenshots on the next page — so stay in the app until it's done.
THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS
After the buyer says they've paid, open GCash, Maya or your bank app and see the money yourself. Only then confirm in Gate. A screenshot from the buyer is not proof, and releasing early is how people get scammed. If the money never arrives, don't confirm — raise a dispute with Gate support instead.

REMEMBER FOR NEXT TIME

01

Match the network

The deposit network on Gate has to match the network you send from. Wrong network, lost ACU.

02

Keep gas back

Never transfer your full ACU balance out of the Hub. Leave a little behind to pay transaction fees.

03

Wait for the deposit

Don't start trading until the ACU has actually landed in your Gate balance. Transfers take a moment.

04

Confirm payment last

In P2P, see the pesos in your own app first, then release. Never the other way round.
